## Login and access instructions

No account is required to browse the homepage, dormitory reviews, cafeteria reviews, record details, or Survival Guide.

Use these fictional demonstration accounts for the complete Lab 14 workflow:

| Role | Email | Password |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | `student@hallpass.com` | `Student123!` |
| Administrator | `admin@hallpass.com` | `Admin123!` |

1. Open the final prototype URL.
2. Browse a listing and open a record detail.
3. Log in as the student to submit a fictional review and view its Pending status.
4. Log out and sign in as administrator to approve or reject the review.
5. Return to the student account to view the updated status.

## Notes for lecturer

- HallPass is a frontend classroom MVP; JavaScript must be enabled.
- Prototype data is stored or simulated in the current browser with `localStorage`.
- Data is not synchronized across browsers or devices, and clearing site data resets locally created records.
- Font Awesome icons load from a public CDN, so an internet connection is recommended.
- Use fictional information only; do not enter personal or sensitive data.
- The prototype demonstrates student discovery, search/filter, record details, review submission, validation and consent, status tracking, dashboards, administrator moderation, reports, user management, and audit evidence.
