# Success Metrics and Decision Rules

## 1. Success Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric                                 |             Target | How to Measure                                                                                 | Related Requirement |
| --------- | -------------------------------------- | -----------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| M-01      | Homepage understanding rate            |      80% or higher | Users who correctly explain the purpose of HallPass / total users                              | FR-01, FR-02        |
| M-02      | Dormitory review browsing success rate |      80% or higher | Users who successfully find a relevant dormitory review / total users                          | FR-05               |
| M-03      | Search or filter success rate          |      80% or higher | Users who successfully find relevant campus information using search or filters / total users  | FR-06               |
| M-04      | Review detail understanding rate       |      80% or higher | Users who correctly understand the information presented on a review detail page / total users | FR-07               |
| M-05      | Review submission success rate         |      80% or higher | Users who successfully complete and submit a review / total users                              | FR-08, FR-10, FR-14 |
| M-06      | Overall usability rating               | Average 4 out of 5 | Average rating collected through the tester feedback form                                      | FR-13               |
| M-07      | Feature usefulness rating              | Average 4 out of 5 | Average rating collected through the tester feedback form                                      | MVP features        |

## 2. Decision Rules

| Result              | Decision                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Metrics meet target | Continue to the implementation sprint and further prototype development.                                          |
| Some metrics fail   | Revise the related interface, navigation flow, labels, or feature priority before implementation.                 |
| Most metrics fail   | Revisit the requirements, user stories, critical assumptions, and problem evidence before continuing development. |
