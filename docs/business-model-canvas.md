# Business Model Canvas

## Project Title

**HallPass – Student Reviews & Campus Survival Guide**

---

## 1. Customer Segments

The main users of HallPass are:

- **First-year university students** looking for reliable information about dormitories, cafeterias, and campus life.
- **International and exchange students** who need guidance to adapt quickly to a new campus environment.
- **Senior students** who contribute reviews, ratings, and campus survival tips based on their experiences.
- **Campus administrators** who review submitted content and ensure information remains accurate and appropriate.

---

## 2. Value Proposition

HallPass provides a single, student-driven platform where new students can easily find trustworthy and up-to-date information about campus life.

The platform helps students:

- Read genuine student reviews of dormitories and cafeterias.
- Access campus survival guides in one place.
- Search for useful information quickly instead of relying on scattered sources.
- Share experiences to help future students make informed decisions.
- Reduce confusion and adapt to university life more confidently.

---

## 3. Channels

Users can find, access, and use HallPass through:

- University website
- Mobile-friendly web application
- GitHub Pages prototype
- QR codes during student orientation
- University social media pages
- Student clubs and orientation events
- Word-of-mouth recommendations from students

---

## 4. Customer Relationships

HallPass maintains interaction with users through:

- Self-service registration and login
- Student review and rating submissions
- Search and filter features
- Campus survival guide updates
- Administrator review and moderation
- Feedback and reporting functions
- Continuous community contributions from students

---

## 5. Revenue Streams / Sustainability Logic

HallPass is designed as a university-supported platform rather than a commercial product.

Its sustainability comes from:

- Improving the onboarding experience for new students.
- Reducing repetitive questions directed to university staff.
- Supporting student engagement through community knowledge sharing.
- Providing a centralized source of reliable campus information.
- Supporting the university's digital transformation initiatives.

Future opportunities may include university sponsorships, partnerships with campus facilities, or promotional listings for approved campus services.

---

## 6. Key Resources

The resources required include:

- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript web application
- GitHub repository and GitHub Pages hosting
- Student-generated reviews and ratings
- Campus survival guide content
- User accounts and administrator dashboard
- Development team
- Testing and documentation resources

---

## 7. Key Activities

The main activities include:

- Developing and maintaining the web platform
- Collecting and displaying student reviews
- Managing dormitory and cafeteria information
- Publishing campus survival guides
- Moderating submitted reviews
- Updating campus information
- Testing and improving the prototype

---

## 8. Key Partners

Potential partners include:

- University Administration
- Student Affairs Office
- University IT Department
- Student Council
- Orientation Committee
- Dormitory Management
- Campus Cafeteria Operators
- Senior student volunteers

---

## 9. Cost Structure

Expected costs include:

- Prototype development time
- GitHub hosting (free for the MVP)
- Website maintenance
- System testing and debugging
- Documentation preparation
- Administrator moderation
- Future cloud database or hosting services if the project expands

---

## Final Note

This Business Model Canvas directly supports the HallPass MVP by aligning its customer segments, value proposition, and operational model with the project's identified user needs. The canvas reflects the MVP features, including browsing dormitory and cafeteria reviews, searching and filtering information, reading campus survival guides, submitting reviews, and administrator moderation. These elements work together to deliver a practical prototype that addresses the problem identified during customer discovery while remaining consistent with the project's system requirements, user stories, and product concept.
