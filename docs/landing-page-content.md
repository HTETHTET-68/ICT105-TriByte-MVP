# Landing Page Content

## Project Title

HallPass — Your Shortcut to Campus Life

## Target User

HallPass is designed for Rangsit University students, especially new, international, and exchange students who need reliable information about accommodation, food, and everyday campus life.

## Headline

Your shortcut to campus life.

## Subheadline

Find student-written dormitory and cafeteria reviews, practical campus guidance, and the information you need in one place—without searching through scattered group chats and social-media posts.

## Problem Statement

New, international, and exchange students often struggle to find clear and trustworthy information about dormitories, affordable food, transportation, banking, visa matters, emergencies, and other parts of daily campus life. The information they need is usually spread across social-media posts, group chats, and word of mouth, which makes comparison difficult and can lead to slow or uncertain decisions. HallPass responds to this validated need by bringing useful student experiences and practical campus information together in one clear, searchable platform.

## Solution Description

The HallPass prototype helps students explore campus-life information, compare dormitories, discover cafeteria options, read practical student guides, and share their own experiences through reviews. It gives users a simpler way to make informed decisions and prepare for life at Rangsit University.

## Key Features

| Feature | Requirement ID | User Value | Prototype Screen/Module |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare dormitories | FR-01 | Users can compare ratings, prices, distance, Wi-Fi, safety, and facilities before choosing accommodation. | `dorms.html` |
| Discover campus food | FR-02 | Users can search cafeteria options, view student ratings, and check practical details before visiting. | `cafeteria.html` |
| Access student-life guidance | FR-03 | Users can find practical information about transportation, banking, emergencies, visa needs, and daily campus life. | `guide.html` |
| Submit and manage reviews | FR-04 | Signed-in users can share experiences, follow moderation status, and manage their submissions. | `write-review.html` |
| Create an account with consent | FR-05 | Users can review the consent flow before creating an account and submitting information. | `signup.html` |

## Benefits

HallPass saves students time by placing essential campus information in one location. It helps users compare options more easily, learn from other students’ experiences, and make more confident decisions about accommodation, food, and everyday campus needs. It is particularly useful for newcomers who may not yet have a local support network or know where to find reliable information.

## Call-to-Action

**Try the HallPass Demo**

Open the prototype, explore the student workflow, and share feedback about what would make campus life easier.

## Responsible Data Message

HallPass only asks users to provide information needed for account consent, reviews, and saved activity. In the current prototype, account details, consent choices, reviews, and saved activity remain in the user’s browser through local storage and are not transmitted to an external server. Page views and call-to-action clicks are also counted locally for classroom demonstration. Users should not enter passwords, financial details, identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive personal information.
