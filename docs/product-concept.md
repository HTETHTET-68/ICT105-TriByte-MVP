# Product Concept

## 1. Project Title
HallPass – Student Reviews & Campus Survival Guide

## 2. Target User
Primary users: international students, first-year students, and exchange students who need reliable information about campus life.

Secondary users: experienced students who contribute reviews, ratings, and campus survival tips to help new students.

## 3. Problem Statement
New students often struggle to find trustworthy and up-to-date information about dormitories, cafeterias, and campus life because existing information is scattered across university websites, social media, and word-of-mouth recommendations.

## 4. Proposed IT Product
A web-based platform that allows students to browse dormitory and cafeteria reviews, read campus survival guides, search for useful information, and contribute their own experiences and ratings to help future students make informed decisions.

## 5. Core Value Proposition
HallPass provides a single, student-driven platform where new students can easily access reliable campus information, reducing confusion and helping them adapt to university life more quickly and confidently.

## 6. MVP Scope
The MVP will focus on:
- User registration and login
- Browse dormitory reviews
- Browse cafeteria reviews
- Read campus survival guides
- Search for reviews and guides
- Submit reviews and ratings
- Admin approval and management of submitted content

## 7. Out of Scope for Current Semester
- Mobile application (Android/iOS)
- AI-based personalized recommendations
- Real-time chat or messaging between students
- Integration with university student information systems
- Multi-language translation support
- Location-based navigation and campus maps
- Gamification and reward system
