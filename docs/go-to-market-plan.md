# HallPass Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product

**HallPass — Student Reviews & Campus Survival Guide**

## Target Early Users

The first test audience is first-year, international, and exchange students at
Rangsit University. These users face the problem most directly when choosing a
dormitory, finding affordable food, and learning unfamiliar campus procedures.
Senior students are a secondary audience because they can contribute reviews
and practical guidance.

## Main Value Proposition

HallPass helps new Rangsit University students make faster, more confident
campus decisions by collecting student experiences and practical advice in one
clear place.

## Launch Sequence

1. Publish the landing page with working links to the HallPass prototype.
2. Test the complete route from the landing page to the prototype.
3. Use one tagged landing-page link or QR code for each outreach channel.
4. Share the landing page with class groups and the international-student community.
5. Promote the landing page through QR posters, Instagram, and student ambassadors.
6. Record landing-page visitors, CTA clicks, demo views, and feedback responses daily.
7. Calculate CTA click rate and conversion rate using the agreed formulas.
8. Compare channel results and improve the headline, CTA, message, or prototype navigation.

## Success Criteria for the First Test

The go-to-market success criteria use the same targets and definitions as
`docs/acquisition-metrics.md`.

| Metric ID | Metric | Formula / Counting Method | Success Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-01 | Landing Page Visitors | Count users who open the HallPass landing page | At least 50 visitors |
| M-02 | CTA Click Rate | `CTA clicks ÷ landing-page visitors × 100` | At least 40% |
| M-03 | Prototype Demo Views | Count users who successfully open the HallPass prototype | At least 30 demo views |
| M-04 | Feedback Responses | Count submitted feedback forms or recorded responses | At least 10 responses |
| M-05 | Conversion Rate | `prototype demo views ÷ landing-page visitors × 100` | At least 25% |

These are targets for the initial outreach experiment, not claims of current
HallPass performance.

## Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Users misunderstand the current limitations of HallPass | Clearly explain what information HallPass provides and avoid unsupported claims. |
| Users submit sensitive or harmful information | Display a data-minimization message and discourage sensitive personal content. |
| The message attracts users outside the intended segment | Lead with “Rangsit University” and tailor each message to a defined student group. |
| Link traffic cannot be attributed to a channel | Use a different tagged landing-page link or QR code for each channel. |
| Interest is high but prototype use is low | Test clearer CTA wording and link directly to the most relevant workflow. |
| A small sample is treated as proof of market demand | Report the sample size and collection method and describe findings as early evidence. |

## Review Decision

After the first test, the team will compare the observed results with M-01 to
M-05. A metric will be marked successful when it meets or exceeds its stated
target. If a metric is below target, the team will:

- Improve the landing-page headline or feature descriptions if visitor interest is low.
- Make the CTA more visible or revise its wording if the CTA click rate is below 40%.
- Simplify the route to the prototype if demo views are below 30.
- Make the feedback request shorter and more visible if responses are below 10.
- Improve the landing-page-to-prototype journey if conversion is below 25%.

The team will prioritize the channel that produces the strongest combination of
demo views, feedback responses, and conversion rate.
