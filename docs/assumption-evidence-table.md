# Assumption-Evidence Table

**Course:** ICT105  
**Team:** TriByte  
**Project:** HallPass – Student Reviews & Campus Survival Guide  
**Evidence Base:** 20 survey responses

| No. | Team Assumption | Evidence Collected | Evidence Type | Strength | Prototype Decision |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First-year, international, and exchange students struggle to find campus-life information. | 14 out of 20 respondents were first-year, international, or exchange students, and they rated the difficulty highly. | Survey | Strong | Keep target user focus. |
| 2 | Campus information is scattered across too many channels. | 8 out of 20 selected scattered information as their biggest problem. | Survey | Strong | Keep HallPass as one centralized platform. |
| 3 | Students spend too much time searching for basic information. | 13 out of 20 spent 30 minutes or more searching for campus information. | Survey | Strong | Emphasize fast browsing and clear navigation. |
| 4 | Dorm and cafeteria reviews are important to new students. | Dorm reviews and cafeteria reviews were among the most requested features. | Survey | Moderate/Strong | Implement `dorms.html` and `cafeteria.html`. |
| 5 | First-week guide content is also important. | First-week guide received similar interest to cafeteria reviews. | Survey | Moderate | Implement `guide.html`. |
| 6 | Students need information written by real students. | Respondents reported that existing information was not always honest or student-based. | Survey | Strong | Include student review cards and write review flow. |
| 7 | Students would use HallPass if available. | 16 out of 20 respondents said Yes and 4 said Maybe. | Survey | Strong | Continue with HallPass MVP. |
| 8 | Review quality needs moderation. | Student-written content may become unreliable without checking. | Team analysis | Moderate | Implement `dashboard.html` as admin moderation view. |

## Summary
The evidence supports the current prototype direction: homepage, dorm reviews, cafeteria reviews, review details, write review form, survival guide, account flow, and admin dashboard.
