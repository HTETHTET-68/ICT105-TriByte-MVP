# HallPass Risk Register

## Rating Rule

- **Severity:** High = significant harm, privacy/security breach, or core feature failure; Medium = limited harm or usability/trust impact; Low = minor impact.
- **Likelihood:** High = likely without controls; Medium = reasonably possible; Low = unlikely in the current classroom prototype.

| Risk ID | Category | Risk Description | Affected Feature / Requirement | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Action | Owner | GitHub Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Privacy | A user may include an email, phone number, student ID, or other personal information in a public review. | F08; FR-08, FR-10, FR-14; NFR-08 | High | Medium | Add a privacy notice beside the form, prohibit personal data, validate content, moderate before publication, and show a username/anonymous label rather than email. | Content Moderator | `prototype/write-review.html`; `docs/data-handling-policy.md`; `data/data-inventory.csv` | In Progress |

## Overall Risk Decision

**Continue with mitigation for classroom prototyping; production deployment is not approved.** HallPass can proceed to the next lab because its current data is fictional or locally simulated and no live backend is connected. Before any real implementation, the team must close R-04 by protecting administrator access and replacing demo authentication, implement moderation and input controls for R-01/R-02/R-06, complete asset licensing evidence for R-03, and complete privacy notice, consent, retention, and deletion procedures for R-05.
