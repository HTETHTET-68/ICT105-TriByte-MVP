# Lab 08 - MVP Decision

## 1. MVP Decision

**Decision:** ✅ **Continue with Minor Revisions**

Based on the customer validation results, the team decided to continue developing the HallPass MVP with minor usability improvements. The prototype successfully met the predefined success metrics and demonstrated that it addresses the needs of first-year, international, and exchange students. The identified issues mainly involve interface usability rather than the overall product concept.

---

## 2. Evidence Supporting the Decision

The decision is supported by the following validation evidence:

- The MVP achieved an **85% task success rate**, exceeding the project target of 80%.
- Users rated the prototype with an average **Ease of Use score of 3.99/5**, indicating that most users could complete the required tasks with little assistance.
- The average **Usefulness score was 4.30/5**, showing that students found HallPass valuable for finding dormitory reviews, cafeteria reviews, and campus guidance.
- The average **Interest Level was 4.34/5**, suggesting that participants would be willing to use HallPass if it were available.
- The most common issues were related to login/signup navigation, search visibility, and review detail presentation. These are usability improvements rather than major problems with the overall solution.

The validation results indicate that the MVP direction is appropriate and should continue with targeted interface improvements.

## 4. Requirements Needing Improvement

Although the MVP achieved positive validation results, several requirements require usability improvements.

| Requirement ID | Evidence | Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| FR-02 | Some users experienced difficulty locating pages and understanding navigation labels. | Improve menu organization and navigation clarity. |
| FR-03 | Login and signup produced the highest confusion during testing. | Simplify the authentication workflow and improve page transitions. |
| FR-06 | Search and filter controls were not immediately noticeable. | Make search and filter controls more prominent. |
| FR-07 | Users requested clearer ratings and review summaries. | Improve the visual hierarchy of review cards and detail pages. |
| FR-08 | Users requested additional review categories and clearer submission guidance. | Expand review categories and improve form instructions. |

---

## 5. Next Prototype Iteration

Before continuing development, the team will focus on the following improvements:

1. Simplify the login and signup workflow.
2. Improve navigation labels and menu visibility.
3. Enhance the search and filter interface.
4. Improve the layout of review detail pages by highlighting ratings and summaries.
5. Expand dormitory, cafeteria, and campus guide content.
6. Improve review submission guidance and validation messages.

These improvements will be implemented before conducting further prototype validation.

## 7. Conclusion

The customer validation confirms that **HallPass successfully addresses its core objective of helping students access reliable dormitory reviews, cafeteria reviews, and campus guidance.** The prototype exceeded the target task success rate and received positive usability, usefulness, and interest ratings from participants.

Rather than redesigning the system, the team will continue with minor usability improvements focused on navigation, authentication, search functionality, and review presentation before moving to the next stage of development.
