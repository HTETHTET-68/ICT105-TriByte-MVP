The problem

Campus answers are everywhere—except where you need them.

New, international, and exchange students often depend on scattered social-media posts, group chats, and word of mouth to compare accommodation, find affordable food, and understand daily campus life.

HallPass brings those student experiences into one clear, searchable platform so users can make faster, more confident decisions.
