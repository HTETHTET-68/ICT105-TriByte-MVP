## Notes

The HallPass landing page introduces the main problem, target users, value proposition, and key features of the project. It is designed for first-year, international, and exchange students who need reliable information about dormitories, cafeterias, and campus life.

The **Try Demo** call-to-action connects visitors to the final HallPass prototype. From the prototype, users can browse dormitory and cafeteria reviews, read campus survival guides, search and filter information, submit reviews, and explore the student and administrator workflows.

The landing page therefore acts as the promotional entry point, while the final prototype demonstrates the actual features and user experience of the HallPass MVP.
